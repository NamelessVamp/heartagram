Checked on 2026-10-26 for the "Trucos y secretos" section of the guide in `index.html`.
`tests/guide-tips-qa.mjs` locks the tip count and the topics below. Re-check this list
after major Switch 2 system updates (several features arrived in 20.1.0 and 22.0.0).

Official Nintendo support pages were readable only through search summaries (direct
fetches returned 403). Spanish menu names are translated from the English ones, so confirm
them on a console set to Spanish (Mexico) before the gift.

## Removed

- "Skip the user selection screen": only documented for the original Switch, and only with a single profile. Nothing confirms it on the Switch 2.

## Left out of the video's 25

Not confirmed by Nintendo's pages or not suitable for this guide.

- Bluetooth audio shortcut inside Quick Settings: the official Quick Settings list has no such item.
- Swapping A and B system-wide: reported by outlets, no official page found.
- Remapping the C button: not confirmed.
- Unpairing every controller with the sync button, and mixing a Joy-Con with a Joy-Con 2: single or no sources.
- Charging a phone from the console: no evidence the console outputs power for it.
- Accessibility extras (text size, bold, contrast, screen reader): the screen reader needs system 22.0.0 and its Spanish support is unconfirmed.
- Deal channels and the giveaway: promotion. DekuDeals stays as the community tip.
