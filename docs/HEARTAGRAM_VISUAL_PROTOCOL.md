# HEARTAGRAM Visual Protocol

## Purpose
HEARTAGRAM is a personal game-discovery gift built by Inti for Adriana.
The interface must feel intimate, discoverable, dark-romantic, and alive.
It must never become a generic store, corporate dashboard, or imitation of another assistant.

## Product principle
- Inti creates the system.
- Adriana inhabits the experience.
- Current intent and usability come before decoration.
- Visual personality supports discovery, collection, shared play, and the gift reveal.

## Visual layers
1. Love Metal: black, wine, magenta, aged gold, warm ivory.
2. Dark Y2K: mono labels, HUD lines, restrained scanlines, system states.
3. Digital Scrapbook: covers, stamps, dot art, personal notes, deliberate asymmetry.
4. Gift layer: warmth, reveal, intimacy, and shared-play language.

## Global vocabulary
- `⟦ LABEL ⟧` for system and section labels.
- `//` for editorial subtitles.
- `✦` for special or pinned items.
- `→` for flow and decisions.
- `♡` for interest and connection.
- `◈` for owned items and artifacts.
- Relevant emoji may be used in moderation.
- SVG icons are used for permanent navigation and controls.
- Braille dot art is used for empty, loading, and ceremonial states.

## Density
- Utility screens remain clear and restrained.
- Emotional screens may be ceremonial.
- Empty states should contain one meaningful visual anchor.
- Decoration must never obscure controls or reduce contrast.

## Screen intent
- Inicio: opening the gift.
- Test: signal detection, not a corporate form.
- Perfil: player signal or identity card.
- Descubrir: editorial recommendation spread.
- Explorar: practical visual archive.
- Mi lista: personal relic collection.
- Regalo: primary emotional reveal.
- Grupo familiar: shared-play connection.

## Motion
Motion supports interaction and presence. No constant decorative motion.
Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Accessibility
- Visible text remains available beside icons where space permits.
- Icon-only controls require accessible labels.
- Color is never the only state indicator.
- Touch targets remain at least 44px.
- Decorative dot art is hidden from assistive technology.
